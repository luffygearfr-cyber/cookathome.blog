About Cook at Home

Where Family Traditions Begin

Welcome to Cook at Home! We believe that the best meals are made with love, shared with family, and created from recipes that have stood the test of time.

Our Story

Cook at Home was born in a small farmhouse kitchen, surrounded by the aroma of freshly baked bread and the warmth of family gathered around the table. What started as a collection of handwritten recipe cards passed down from grandmothers has grown into something we're incredibly proud to share with you.

We believe that cooking at home shouldn't be complicated or intimidating. It should be joyful, nourishing, and accessible to everyone – whether you're a complete beginner or a seasoned home cook looking for new inspiration.

Meet Sarah

I'm Sarah Mitchell – home cook, recipe developer, and the heart behind Cook at Home. I grew up watching my grandmother transform simple ingredients into magical meals that brought our whole family together.

Now, I'm on a mission to help families everywhere create their own food memories. When I'm not in the kitchen, you'll find me at the farmers market, tending my herb garden, or convincing my kids that yes, they really do need to try just one bite.

Our Promise to You

Every recipe on Cook at Home is:

  • Thoroughly Tested: We make every recipe ourselves, often multiple times, before sharing
  • Clearly Written: Step-by-step instructions that anyone can follow
  • Honest: Real prep times, real ingredients, real results
  • Family-Friendly: Recipes the whole family will actually eat and enjoy
