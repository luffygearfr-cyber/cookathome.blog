Common Questions

Can I share your recipes on my blog or social media?

We'd be honored! Please link back to the original recipe and credit Cook at Home. You may use one photo with proper attribution.

Can I substitute ingredients in your recipes?

Absolutely! We include substitution suggestions when possible. If you're unsure about a specific swap, email us and we'll help.

My recipe didn't turn out right. What happened?

Don't worry – it happens to all of us! Send us details about what went wrong and we'll help troubleshoot.

Do you accept guest posts?

We occasionally feature guest recipes from fellow home cooks. Email us your idea!
